What's Actually in a Myers Cocktail

People ask us this a lot, so here's the straightforward version - our Classic Myers Cocktail combines IV fluids with four core ingredients:

Vitamin C

An antioxidant that also plays a role in immune function and collagen production.

B-Complex Vitamins

A group of B vitamins involved in energy metabolism and nervous system function.

Calcium

Supports muscle function and bone health, alongside the other nutrients in the blend.

Magnesium

Involved in hundreds of processes in the body, including muscle relaxation and nerve function.

Every clinic's version varies slightly - ours is dosed and mixed by our medical team, not a fixed off-the-shelf recipe.
