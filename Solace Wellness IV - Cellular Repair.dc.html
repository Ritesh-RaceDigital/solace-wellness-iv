What's Actually Happening

How Your Cells Repair Themselves

Every day, your cells accumulate damage - from UV exposure, metabolic byproducts, stress, and just the normal business of being alive. Your body has sophisticated mechanisms to manage this, including sirtuin enzymes that depend on NAD+, and antioxidant systems that run on Glutathione.

The challenge is that both NAD+ and Glutathione naturally decline as you age, and can be further depleted by chronic stress, poor sleep, and other lifestyle factors. When these levels drop, your cells' ability to repair themselves takes a hit too.

IV delivery gets these compounds into your bloodstream directly - no digestion step, no absorption loss. Your cells get access to what they need immediately.

NAD+ & Sirtuins

Sirtuin proteins regulate cellular health and stress response - and they can't function without adequate NAD+. Replenishing it supports this whole system.

Glutathione & Detox

Often called the body's master antioxidant, Glutathione helps your liver clear toxins and neutralizes the free radicals behind cellular aging.

Why IV Delivery

Both NAD+ and Glutathione are largely broken down before they can be absorbed orally. Direct IV delivery bypasses that problem entirely.
