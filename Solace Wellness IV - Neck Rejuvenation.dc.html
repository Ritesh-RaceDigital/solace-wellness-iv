What It Supports

Why Choose Neck Rejuvenation?

Xeomin and Dysport are neurotoxin treatments that temporarily relax the platysma - the thin, sheet-like muscle running down the front of the neck. Relaxing it softens the vertical bands and lines that show when the muscle pulls or tenses.

Softer Platysmal Bands

Relaxes the vertical neck bands that show when you clench your jaw or make a "grimace" expression.

Smoother Neck Lines

Eases horizontal creases across the neck that can deepen with repeated movement over time.

More Defined Jawline

By relaxing the downward pull of the platysma, the jawline can look a touch more lifted and defined.

Quick, Low-Downtime Visit

A series of small injections along the neck, done in about 15-20 minutes, with no real recovery time.

Worth Asking Yourself

Is Neck Rejuvenation Right for You?

Neck changes tend to happen gradually, so it's easy to not notice until you catch a certain angle in a photo. A few things our clients mention before their first visit:

Vertical bands that show when you clench your jaw or make a "grimace" expression in the mirror

Horizontal lines across the neck that bother you in photos

A jawline that feels less defined than it used to

Wanting a non-surgical option before considering something more involved

None of this is a diagnosis - if it sounds familiar, mention it during your consultation and we'll talk through whether this treatment makes sense for you.

The Appointment

What Actually Happens During Your Visit

1. Consultation & Health Screening

We'll look at how your platysma moves when you clench and talk through your goals, plus review your health history.

2. The Injections

A series of small injections along the platysma muscle with a very fine needle - most clients describe each one as a quick pinch.

3. Back to Your Day

No numbing required for most clients, no real downtime. Light redness or small bumps at the injection sites usually settle within an hour.

4. Results Build In

You'll start noticing softening within 3-7 days, with full results visible by about two weeks.

Who It's For

Anyone bothered by visible platysmal bands or neck lines who wants a quick, non-surgical way to smooth the neck without downtime.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for those with a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, or with a known allergy to the product or its ingredients. A health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Avoid lying down, rubbing the treated area, or strenuous exercise for about 4 hours afterward. Skip alcohol that day if you can - it can make bruising more noticeable.
