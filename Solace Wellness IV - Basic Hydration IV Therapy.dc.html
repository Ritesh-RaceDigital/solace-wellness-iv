Who It's For

Anyone who just needs straightforward fluids and electrolytes - mild dehydration, a busy Vegas day, or first-timers who want to try IV therapy without the full vitamin blend.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended for those who are pregnant or nursing, or who have certain kidney, heart, or liver conditions. A brief health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

No downtime - most clients return to their day immediately after the infusion.
