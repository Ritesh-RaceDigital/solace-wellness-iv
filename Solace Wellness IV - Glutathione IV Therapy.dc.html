A Little More Detail

What Oxidative Stress Actually Means

"Oxidative stress" gets thrown around a lot in wellness circles, so here's the plain-language version: it's the wear and tear your cells accumulate from everyday life - sun exposure, alcohol, pollution, poor sleep, even normal aging.

The Body's Master Antioxidant

Glutathione is produced by nearly every cell and helps neutralize the unstable molecules (free radicals) behind oxidative stress.

Why Levels Drop

Production naturally slows with age, and can be further depleted by chronic stress, illness, or poor diet.

Why an Injection

Oral glutathione is broken down during digestion before much of it is absorbed - an injection delivers it directly.

Where Skin Fits In

Because oxidative stress shows up visibly in skin, replenishing glutathione is one reason clients notice a brighter complexion.
