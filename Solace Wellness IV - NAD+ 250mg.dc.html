Why Start Here

250mg Is the Right First Step

NAD+ naturally declines as you age - research suggests levels can drop by roughly half between your 20s and 50s. That drop is gradual enough that most people don't notice until they're well into it. That's usually when they start looking for answers.

The 250mg dose gives your cells a meaningful top-up without rushing the process. Since NAD+ infusions need to run slowly to stay comfortable, starting at a lower dose also makes your first experience a more relaxed one - no one wants to feel overwhelmed on a first visit.

A lot of clients stay at 250mg as their regular maintenance dose. Others move up to 500mg once they've had a session or two and know how they respond. There's no single right answer - it depends on your goals and how your body reacts.

What NAD+ Does in Your Cells

It's a coenzyme your mitochondria need to convert nutrients into energy. It also plays a role in DNA repair and how cells handle day-to-day wear.

Why 250mg for Beginners

Why IV, Not Supplements

Oral NAD+ is largely broken down before it reaches your bloodstream. An IV infusion delivers it directly - no digestive step in between.
