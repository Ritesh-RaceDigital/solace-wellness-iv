Meet Our Founder

Founded by a Board-Certified Nurse Practitioner

Founded by board-certified nurse practitioner Christina, Solace Wellness IV was created to bring a more personalized and medically guided approach to IV vitamin therapy.

After years caring for patients in hospitals and serving in the United States Air Force medical field, she recognized the need for wellness care focused on prevention, education, and individualized treatment - rather than one-size-fits-all services.

Today, patients can receive care in our Summerlin wellness suite or through our convenient mobile IV service throughout Las Vegas.

— Christina, NP · Founder, Solace Wellness IV
