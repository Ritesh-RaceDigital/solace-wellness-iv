What It Supports

Why Choose Single Area Neurotoxin Treatment?

Xeomin and Dysport are both neurotoxin treatments that temporarily relax the small muscles behind dynamic wrinkles - the lines that show up when you raise your eyebrows or furrow your forehead. Treating a single area keeps things simple, natural-looking, and easy to fit into a first visit.

Softer Forehead Lines

The most common reason clients book this treatment. A few small injections relax the muscles that create those horizontal lines across the forehead.

Natural, Not Frozen

Dosed for one area, results tend to look like a well-rested version of you - your expression stays intact, the deepest lines just soften.

Fast Appointment

Most single-area visits take about 15-20 minutes, injections included - easy to fit between errands or on a lunch break.

Little to No Downtime

A little redness at the injection sites is normal and usually fades within an hour. Most clients go straight back to their day.

Common Areas We Treat

"Single area" means we're focusing the treatment on wherever bothers you most. Forehead lines are what most clients come in for, but the same approach works well for a few other spots too.

The Appointment

What Actually Happens During Your Visit

1. Consultation & Health Screening

We'll talk through your goals, review your health history, and map out exactly where the product goes.

2. The Injections

A handful of small injections into the chosen area with a very fine needle - most clients describe it as a quick pinch.

3. Back to Your Day

No numbing required for most clients, no real downtime. Light redness or small bumps at the injection sites usually settle within an hour.

4. Results Build In

You'll start noticing softening within 3-7 days, with full results visible by about two weeks.

Who It's For

Anyone bothered by dynamic wrinkles in one specific spot - most often forehead lines - who wants a quick, natural-looking refresh without committing to a full treatment plan.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for those with a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, or with a known allergy to the product or its ingredients. A health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Avoid lying down, rubbing the treated area, or strenuous exercise for about 4 hours afterward. Skip alcohol that day if you can - it can make bruising more noticeable.
