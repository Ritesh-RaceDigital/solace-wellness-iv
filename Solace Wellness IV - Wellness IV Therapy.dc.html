IV therapy & wellness is the infusion of fluids, vitamins, minerals and nutrients directly into the bloodstream through an intravenous (IV) infusion. Many people choose wellness IV therapy to support hydration, energy, recovery and overall wellness under professional medical care.

With mobile IV therapy you receive the same professional care, but in the comfort of your own home, hotel or office. No need to travel to an IV & wellness lounge - we bring the whole experience to you, delivered by our licensed nurses.

Recovery Room IV Therapy & Wellness is a catch-all for IV treatments that help you rehydrate, recuperate and feel better overall after travel, exercise, long workdays or social events. Your treatment is tailored to your health history and wellness goals.

Most appointments are 45-60 minutes long and include your health screening, IV administration and aftercare instructions.

IV therapy is available for most healthy adults following a medical screening. Before treatment, our licensed medical team reviews your health history and confirms your selected IV therapy is appropriate for your specific needs.
