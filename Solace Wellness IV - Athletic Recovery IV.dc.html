Who It's For

Athletes, marathon runners, CrossFitters, and anyone training hard in the Vegas heat who wants to recover faster between sessions.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended for those who are pregnant or nursing, or who have certain kidney, heart, or liver conditions. A brief health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Stretch, rest, and stay hydrated. Avoid intense training for the rest of the day to let your body fully recover.
