What It Supports

Why Choose Masseter Contouring?

Xeomin and Dysport are neurotoxin treatments that relax targeted muscles. Used in the masseter, that means less bulk in the jaw muscle itself - not just softer movement, like with a wrinkle-focused treatment.

Slimmer Jawline

Relaxing the masseter muscle softens a square or bulky jaw shape over time, for a more tapered, contoured profile.

Less Jaw Tension

Many clients book this treatment for relief from clenching and grinding, not just the cosmetic result.

Gradual, Natural Change

The muscle shrinks slowly over several weeks, so the slimming effect looks like a natural change rather than an overnight transformation.

Quick, Low-Downtime Visit

A handful of small injections per side, done in about 15-20 minutes, with no real recovery time.

Worth Asking Yourself

Is Masseter Contouring Right for You?

A wider or squarer jawline isn't a problem to fix - but if it bothers you, or your jaw is giving you grief, it's worth knowing this option exists. A few things our clients mention before their first visit:

A square or wide jawline that runs in the family or has become more noticeable over time

Frequent jaw soreness, headaches, or tension from clenching or grinding

A visibly bulky jaw muscle when you clench your teeth

Wanting a slimmer, more tapered lower face without surgery

None of this is a diagnosis - if it sounds familiar, mention it during your consultation and we'll talk through whether this treatment makes sense for you.

The Appointment

What Actually Happens During Your Visit

1. Consultation & Health Screening

We'll feel how the masseter muscle engages when you clench, talk through your goals, and review your health history.

2. The Injections

A handful of small injections into the masseter muscle on each side, with a very fine needle - most clients describe it as a quick pinch.

3. Back to Your Day

No numbing required for most clients, no real downtime. Mild soreness when chewing is normal for the first few days.

4. Results Build In

Jaw tension often eases within a week or two. The visible slimming takes longer, building over 4-8 weeks as the muscle gradually shrinks.

Who It's For

Anyone bothered by a square or bulky jawline from an overactive masseter, or dealing with jaw tension from clenching or grinding, who wants a non-surgical way to soften their jawline.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for those with a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, or with a known allergy to the product or its ingredients. A health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Skip vigorous chewing or massaging the area for the rest of the day, and avoid lying down or rubbing the treated spots for about 4 hours afterward.
