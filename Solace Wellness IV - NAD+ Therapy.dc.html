The Science, Simply Put

Why NAD+ Gets So Much Attention

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell in your body. It's a key part of how your mitochondria - the "power plants" inside your cells - actually produce energy. It's also involved in DNA repair and how your cells respond to everyday stress.

Here's the part that gets people's attention: research suggests NAD+ levels can fall by roughly half between your 20s and your 50s. That decline is one of the reasons people start looking into ways to support it directly, rather than just hoping diet and sleep keep up.

An IV infusion delivers NAD+ straight into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system entirely - which is why it's infused slowly rather than all at once.

What NAD+ Does

Powers mitochondrial energy production, supports DNA repair, and helps cells manage everyday wear and tear.

Why It Declines

Natural aging is the biggest factor, though stress, poor sleep, and alcohol use can speed it up too.

Why IV, Not Pills

Oral NAD+ supplements are broken down in digestion before much reaches your bloodstream - an infusion skips that step.
