Who It's For

A good fit if you've been dealing with weeks of low energy, recovering from a stretch of poor sleep or high stress, or coming off a demanding travel or work schedule and need more than a quick pick-me-up.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended for those who are pregnant or nursing, or who have certain kidney, heart, or liver conditions. A brief health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Continue drinking water for the rest of the day, and give yourself permission to rest if you can - the infusion works alongside recovery, not instead of it.
