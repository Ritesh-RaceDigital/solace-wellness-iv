What to Expect

Before, During & After Your Vitamin D3 Booster

Before

A brief health screening is performed to confirm the treatment is appropriate for you. No fasting or special preparation is required.

During

The injection takes approximately 5 minutes and is administered by a licensed medical professional. Most clients feel only a brief pinch.

After

No special aftercare needed - most clients return to their day immediately. Mild soreness at the injection site can occur and typically resolves within a day.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended for those with certain kidney conditions or existing high calcium levels. A brief health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.
