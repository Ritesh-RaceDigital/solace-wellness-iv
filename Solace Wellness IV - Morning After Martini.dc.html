Who It's For

Anyone dealing with dehydration, headache, fatigue, or nausea after a night out on the Strip - this is our most requested treatment for that exact situation.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended for those who are pregnant or nursing, or who have certain kidney, heart, or liver conditions. A brief health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Rest and keep drinking water for the next 24 hours, and hold off on alcohol until you're feeling fully recovered.
