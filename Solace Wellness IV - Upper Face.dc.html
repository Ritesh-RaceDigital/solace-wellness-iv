What It Supports

Why Choose Upper Face Treatment?

Xeomin and Dysport are neurotoxin treatments that temporarily relax the small muscles behind dynamic wrinkles - the lines that show up when you raise your eyebrows, squint, or frown. Treating your whole upper face together keeps the result looking even, instead of one area standing out against the others.

The Appointment

What Actually Happens During Your Visit

1. Consultation & Health Screening

We'll talk through your goals for each area, review your health history, and map out exactly where the product goes.

2. The Injections

A series of small injections across the forehead, crow's feet, and 11 lines with a very fine needle - most clients describe each one as a quick pinch.

3. Back to Your Day

No numbing required for most clients, no real downtime. Light redness or small bumps at the injection sites usually settle within an hour.

4. Results Build In

You'll start noticing softening within 3-7 days, with full results visible by about two weeks.

Who It's For

Anyone who wants their forehead, crow's feet, and 11 lines addressed together for a more even, balanced result - rather than treating just one spot.

Who Should Avoid This

Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for those with a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, or with a known allergy to the product or its ingredients. A health screening confirms whether this treatment is right for you.

Aftercare

Avoid lying down, rubbing the treated areas, or strenuous exercise for about 4 hours afterward. Skip alcohol that day if you can - it can make bruising more noticeable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Upper Face treatment covers the three areas that move the most when you're expressive: forehead lines, crow's feet at the outer corners of the eyes, and the "11" lines between the brows - all treated together in one visit.

"Botox" has become the generic, everyday name people use for any neurotoxin treatment, similar to how "Kleenex" gets used for tissues. We use Xeomin or Dysport for Upper Face treatment - both are neurotoxin injections that work the same way, and we'll recommend which one fits you best at your consultation.

Crow's feet treatment is a neurotoxin injection around the outer corners of the eyes that softens the fine lines that appear when you smile or squint. It's one of the three areas included in Upper Face treatment.

Crow's feet injections use a very fine needle to place small amounts of neurotoxin into the muscles around the eye, temporarily relaxing the movement that causes those lines to form. Most clients describe each injection as a quick pinch.

The "11 lines" (also called glabellar lines) are the two vertical lines that appear between your eyebrows when you frown or concentrate - named for how they resemble the number 11. 11 Lines Treatment relaxes the muscles that create them, softening the lines both at rest and when you make an expression.

Single Area Neurotoxin Treatment focuses on just one spot, most often the forehead. Upper Face treats three areas together - forehead, crow's feet, and the 11 lines - for a more balanced result across your whole upper face.

Most clients see results for about 3-4 months before the treated muscles gradually return to their normal movement. Results tend to last a bit longer with consistent, ongoing treatment.
